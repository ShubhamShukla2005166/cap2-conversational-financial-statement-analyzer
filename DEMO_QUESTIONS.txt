Conversational Financial Statement Analyzer - Demo Questions
============================================================

Tabular upload: data/sample_financial_statement.csv
Unstructured upload: data/large_unstructured_financial_report.txt

Use these short questions for a focused demo.

1. START HERE
-------------
What periods are covered?
Which metrics are available?
Give me an overview of the company.

2. KEY FINANCIAL METRICS
------------------------
Show me revenue for all years.
What is the current ratio?
What is the latest debt-to-equity ratio?
What is the debt ratio?
What is the gross margin?
What is the latest operating margin?
What is the net margin in 2024?
What is the latest DSO?
What is the inventory-to-revenue ratio?
What is cash flow to net income?
What is return on assets?
What is return on equity?

3. TRENDS
---------
How has the current ratio changed?
How has operating margin changed?
How has revenue changed over time?

4. COMPARISONS
--------------
What is the difference in revenue between 2019 and 2024?
What is the difference in net income between 2020 and 2024?

5. RED FLAGS
------------
What are all the active red flags?
Why is liquidity a concern?
Why is leverage a concern?
Why is DSO climbing?
Why is inventory a concern?
Why is margin compression a concern?
Why is accrual quality a concern?

6. FOLLOW-UP DEMO
-----------------
Ask each pair in order to demonstrate conversation context:

What was the revenue in 2022 and 2024?
What is the difference between them?

What is the current ratio?
How has it changed?

What are the active red flags?
Why is leverage a concern?
